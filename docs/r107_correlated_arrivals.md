# Correlated-arrival experiment

> **Release inputs.** The compact daily, bootstrap and figure data are in `results/pressure21_v1/r107/`. The driver creates the correlated draws and trajectories from the formal and fixed-day scale inputs; use a fresh output directory as described below.

The experiment retains all 50 formal 200-EV days (source seeds 65000–65049) at 600 and 800 kW and all three fixed-day 3000-EV days (111000–111002) at 12000 kW. It compares the frozen Pressure21 continuation policy (seed 11002, beta 0.3) using the final incremental scheduler and the existing urgency-proportional allocation (UPA), each with safety postprocessing. It performs no training, expert optimization or resource benchmark. The original run archived inputs, trajectories, random draws, hashes and execution sources in `artifacts/runs/pressure21_v1/r107_correlated/`; the compact results are in `results/pressure21_v1/r107/`. The large archive is not included. The 3000-EV source files are the fixed-day scale inputs whose identity with `generate(seed, n, fixed_day=True)` is verified by `scripts/evaluate_pressure21.py`; they are not the separate historical non-fixed-day timing inputs.

For each original day, keep battery capacity, rated power, arrival and target SoC, efficiency, slot duration, objective weight and prices exactly unchanged. Let `r_i = ceil(capacity_i * (target_i - arrival_i) / (efficiency_i * power_i * dt))`. Compute the exact discrete masses `pA` and `pD` of rounding and clipping normal draws N(8, 4²) to 0–16 and N(40, 4²) to 32–56, as in the fixed-day generator. For each vehicle construct `w_i(a,d) proportional to pA(a) pD(d) 1[d-a >= r_i]`. This is the conditional joint distribution induced by the original independent pair redraws given the vehicle parameters. Each day draws one standard normal `Z`, independent standard normals `epsilon_i`, and independent uniforms `V_i`, then forms `z_i = sqrt(0.5) Z + sqrt(0.5) epsilon_i`. The normal CDF of `z_i` is inverted through the `w_i` arrival marginal; `V_i` is inverted through `w_i(d | a)` for departure. There is no rejection sampling or selection by outcomes. The 0.5 coefficient is the theoretical correlation of the continuous latent normals, not the measured correlation of the discrete arrival slots. Conditional departure sampling can also induce cross-vehicle departure dependence.

Each stream uses `numpy.random.default_rng(SeedSequence([source_seed + 120000, stream_id]))`; IDs 1, 2 and 3 are the common normal, independent vehicle normals and departure uniforms. The common factor, individual normals, latent values, both sets of uniforms, required steps and complete modified inputs are retained. The original input and each output trajectory have SHA-256 digests in `daily.csv`; the frozen execution protocol contains source and model hashes. The inputs are identical across methods and capacities for each day. In a separate working copy without `artifacts/runs/pressure21_v1/r107_correlated/` and `results/pressure21_v1/r107/`, run `uv run python scripts/run_r107_correlated.py`: the driver refuses to replace existing archives. The optional independent reconstruction `uv run python scripts/audit_r107_correlated.py` writes new summaries and refuses existing files.

The initial whole-fleet target feasibility LP is diagnostic only: all days are kept irrespective of policy results. It reports feasible only for a checked primal residual at most 1e-6, infeasible for the solver's infeasible status, and unknown otherwise. All 103 day–capacity inputs were feasible; none were infeasible or unknown, so the all-case and feasible-subset summaries coincide. This initial feasibility result does not ensure an online policy meets departures. Service counts a vehicle as met if its departure SoC shortfall is at most 1e-4; day attainment requires every vehicle to meet this threshold. Battery-side shortfall is the sum of positive departure SoC deficits times battery capacity. C and D follow the existing same-state cost and squared normalized-power allocation definitions. The independent audit recomputes C, D, SoC dynamics, inactive power, station and per-vehicle bounds, initial and upper/lower SoC and target bounds, with maximum combined residual 9.095e-12. Individual target reachability and unchanged input parameters were verified for all 53 inputs.

The daily values, sample standard deviations and paired Ours-minus-UPA statistics appear in `daily.csv` and `summary.csv`; the 3000-EV values should be interpreted as three individual cases, not a precise population estimate. For the 50 days, mean pairwise cross-day Pearson correlations across the 200 fixed vehicle indices are 0.4037 for the continuous latent values and 0.4030 for the realized discrete arrivals. These are descriptive estimates from 50 independent days, not replacements for the specified latent correlation. Across those days, the mean within-day arrival standard deviation is 2.79 slots and the mean largest single-slot arrival fraction is 0.16. For three 3000-EV days, individual input statistics and results are supplied without estimating a stable cross-day correlation.

The 200-EV paired difference confidence intervals use the existing `analyze_evaluation_uncertainty.py` routine with 10,000 paired day bootstrap samples and seed 20261101. `uv run python scripts/analyze_r107_bootstrap.py` generates `bootstrap.json` from the archived daily records and refuses to overwrite it. Ours minus UPA for C and D respectively is -588.46 [95% percentile interval -615.56, -560.02] and -559.81 [-580.20, -539.92] at 800 kW, and -428.33 [-457.08, -398.64] and -307.55 [-329.55, -285.31] at 600 kW. These intervals describe variation across the 50 days for the fixed policy and are not adjusted for multiple comparisons. Relative reductions computed as differences of the two method means divided by the UPA mean are 27.25% and 90.45% at 800 kW, 20.56% and 86.42% at 600 kW, and 24.73% and 88.40% over the three 3000-EV days, for C and D respectively.

All 103 UPA trajectories reached every target within the stated service tolerance while satisfying the audited physical constraints.

The correlated-arrival figure uses the 100 original and correlated 200-EV inputs. It preserves seed order and uses a common 0–25% per-slot scale. `figure_heatmap.csv`, `figure_correlations.csv`, and `figure_manifest.json` contain the plotted numbers and source/output hashes; `figure_correlated_arrivals.png` is the visual preview and `figure_audit.json` records independent count and standardized-cross-product checks. Correlations are descriptive averages over different vehicle-index pairs computed across 50 days; the pairs are not independent replications, and the empirical coefficients are not the prescribed latent correlation.
